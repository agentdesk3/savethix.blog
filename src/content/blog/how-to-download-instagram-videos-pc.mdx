## 1. Copy the Instagram video link

First, open Instagram in your browser and find the video you want to save.

1. Open the post or Reel.
2. Select **Share** or the post menu, depending on the interface.
3. Choose **Copy link** if that option is available.
4. Keep the copied link ready for the next step.

Instagram's interface can change, so the exact menu labels may differ. If you cannot copy a link, check the post in Instagram's website or app.

## 3. Download the video to your PC

When a download option is available:

1. Select the download option.
2. If your browser asks where to save the file, choose a folder you can find again.
3. Otherwise, check your browser's default Downloads folder.
4. Wait for the download to finish before opening the file.

On Windows, you can usually open the Downloads folder from File Explorer. In Chrome or another browser, you can also open the downloads list to see the file and its location.

## 4. Find the downloaded video

If you cannot find the file, check these places:

- **Browser downloads:** Open the browser's downloads list and look for the most recent file.
- **Windows Downloads folder:** Open File Explorer and select Downloads.
- **A custom folder:** If you chose a location when saving, check that folder.
- **File extension:** Look at the downloaded file's name and extension to identify its format.

If the download is incomplete or shows an error, remove only the incomplete copy if you no longer need it, then retry when the original video is still accessible.

## 5. Troubleshoot common download problems

### The Instagram link does not work

Copy the link again from the original post and make sure the full URL was pasted without extra text. Confirm that the post still opens normally in Instagram.

### The video is private or unavailable

A downloader may not be able to access private, deleted, age-restricted, or otherwise restricted content. Respect the account's privacy settings and do not try to bypass access controls.

### The download option does not appear

The service may not support that particular post or may be temporarily unable to retrieve it. Try a different publicly accessible video to help determine whether the issue is specific to the original post.

### The download is slow or fails

Check your internet connection, wait for any current request to finish, and try again later. Your browser may also block a download or ask you to confirm it.

### The video does not play

Check that the download finished and that your media player supports the file's format. If the file is incomplete, try downloading it again when the source remains available.
