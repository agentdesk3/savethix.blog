Want to save a TikTok video to your iPhone? The method depends on whether the creator allows downloads and where you want to keep the video. This guide covers TikTok's built-in option, an alternative for supported public links, and where to find downloaded files.

## Method 1: Use TikTok's built-in download option

1. Open TikTok on your iPhone.
2. Open the video you want to save.
3. Tap **Share**.
4. If available, tap **Save video**.
5. Open the Photos app and check your recent videos.

The **Save video** option may not appear for every post. Creators can control whether other people are allowed to download their videos.

## Where do downloaded videos go on iPhone?

The destination depends on how the file was downloaded.

- **Safari downloads:** Open the Files app and check **Downloads**, usually under iCloud Drive or On My iPhone.
- **Save to Photos:** If the downloaded video is in Files, open it, tap the Share button, and look for **Save Video**.
- **Can't find the file?** Check Safari's download list and the Downloads folder in Files.

## Troubleshooting common problems

### The Save video button is missing

The creator may have disabled downloads for that video. Try another video or use a supported link-based method if you have permission to save it.

### The download does not start

Check that the link was copied correctly, your connection is working, and the video is still available. Try opening the original post in TikTok again and copying its link.

### The video does not appear in Photos

Check the Files app first. A browser download may be stored there instead of being added automatically to Photos.

### The downloaded file will not play

Try downloading it again and check whether the file finished downloading. If the problem continues, the source may be unavailable or the file may be incomplete.

## Frequently asked questions

### Can I download every TikTok video on my iPhone?

No. Availability depends on the creator's settings, the post's accessibility, and the method used.

### Does downloading a video automatically save it to Photos?

Not always. Browser downloads often go to Files first. You may need to open the file and choose **Save Video**.

### Can I repost a downloaded TikTok video?

Downloading a video does not give you ownership or permission to republish it. Get permission from the rights holder and respect applicable platform rules and copyright law.
