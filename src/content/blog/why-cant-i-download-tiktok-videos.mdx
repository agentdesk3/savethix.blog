Having trouble downloading a TikTok video? The problem may be related to the video's privacy settings, an unavailable post, an incorrect link, your internet connection, or a temporary service issue. This guide walks through practical checks to help you identify the cause.

## 1. Check whether the TikTok video is still available

Open the original video in the TikTok app or website.

- If the video no longer opens, it may have been deleted or restricted.
- If TikTok asks you to sign in, access may depend on your account.
- If the post is private or otherwise restricted, a link-based downloader may not be able to access it.

A downloader cannot reliably process a video that it cannot access. Respect privacy settings and do not try to bypass access restrictions.

## 2. Copy the video link again

An incomplete or incorrect link can cause a download to fail.

1. Open the video in TikTok.
2. Tap **Share**.
3. Choose **Copy link**.
4. Open the downloader and paste the newly copied link.
5. Check that you pasted the entire link, without extra text or spaces.

If the link still fails, open the original post again to confirm that it remains available.

## 3. Check your internet connection

A weak or interrupted connection can prevent a video from loading or a download from completing.

- Open another website to check whether your connection works.
- Switch between Wi-Fi and mobile data if possible.
- Temporarily disable a VPN if it appears to be interfering with access.
- Retry after the connection becomes stable.

Avoid repeatedly submitting the same link in quick succession. Wait for the current request to finish before trying again.

## 4. The downloader keeps loading or shows an error

If a supported public link does not process, the service may be temporarily unavailable or unable to access that particular video.

1. Refresh the downloader page.
2. Copy the TikTok link again.
3. Try another publicly accessible video.
4. Wait a little while before retrying.
5. Check whether the original video still plays normally.

If one video fails but another works, the issue may be specific to the original post. If several supported links fail, the service or a wider access issue may be responsible.

## 5. The download starts but never finishes

An interrupted connection, browser issue, or temporary processing problem can stop a download.

- Check your internet connection.
- Look at your browser's download list for an interrupted or failed download.
- Make sure your device has enough free storage.
- Retry the download if the source remains available.

## 6. You cannot find the downloaded video

The file may have been saved somewhere other than your photo gallery.

- **iPhone:** Check Safari's download list and the Downloads folder in the Files app. You may need to use the Share menu to save the file to Photos.
- **Android:** Check your browser's download history and your file manager's Downloads folder.
- **Computer:** Check the Downloads folder configured in your browser.

The exact location depends on your browser, device, and download method.

## 7. The video has a watermark or the wrong format

The result depends on the options actually offered for the video. Do not assume every link will provide every format or a watermark-free file.

Review the available download options before starting. If the result is not what you expected, check whether another supported option is offered.

## Frequently asked questions

### Why does one TikTok video download while another does not?

Videos can differ in availability, privacy settings, restrictions, and how the service can access them. A working link does not guarantee that every other link will work.

### Can I download a private TikTok video?

A private or restricted video may not be accessible to a downloader. Respect the creator's privacy and use only content you are authorized to access.

### Why does my TikTok link say it is invalid?

The link may have been copied incorrectly, the post may no longer be available, or the service may not support that link. Copy it again from the original post and check that the video opens normally.

### What should I do if every download fails?

Check your connection, test a publicly accessible video, and retry later. If multiple supported links still fail, the issue may be temporary or related to the service's access to TikTok.

### Is it okay to repost a downloaded TikTok video?

Downloading does not give you permission to republish someone else's work. Get permission from the rights holder and follow applicable copyright law and platform rules.
