Instagram Stories let people share temporary photos and videos. If you want to keep a Story, you can use Instagram's available save options or try SaveThisX with a supported, publicly accessible Story link.

This guide explains how to download a Story, find the saved file, and troubleshoot common problems.

## 1. Copy the Instagram Story link

1. Open Instagram and locate the Story you want to save.
2. Use Instagram's available sharing options to copy its link.
3. Make sure the Story is still available.
4. Only proceed if you are authorized to access the content.

Stories are temporary. A link may stop working when the Story expires or becomes inaccessible.

## 3. Can you download Stories from private accounts?

Private or restricted content may not be accessible through a public link. SaveThisX does not sign in to Instagram or bypass account restrictions.

Do not attempt to bypass privacy settings. Only access and save content you are authorized to view.

## 4. Save an Instagram Story on iPhone, Android, or a computer

After a successful download, the file location depends on your device and browser.

- **iPhone:** Check Safari's download list or the Downloads folder in the Files app. If the video is saved as a file, use the available sharing options to save it to Photos.
- **Android:** Check your browser's download history or the Downloads folder in your file manager.
- **Windows or Mac:** Check the Downloads folder configured in your browser.

If you cannot find the file, confirm that the download completed and check your browser's download history.

## 5. What if the Instagram Story link does not work?

A Story download can fail if the Story has expired, the link is incomplete, access is restricted, or Instagram no longer makes the content accessible.

Try these steps:

1. Confirm the Story is still available.
2. Copy the link again using Instagram's sharing options.
3. Check your internet connection.
4. Try another supported, publicly accessible Instagram Story link.
5. If other public Instagram videos also fail, read our guide to [fixing Instagram video download problems](/blog/instagram-video-download-not-working/).

Because Stories are temporary, a link that worked earlier may no longer work.

## Frequently asked questions

### Can I download any Instagram Story?

Not necessarily. The Story must still be accessible, you must be authorized to view it, and the link must be supported.

### Does SaveThisX support Instagram Stories?

Yes. SaveThisX supports publicly accessible Instagram Story links, although private, expired, restricted, or otherwise inaccessible content may not download.

### Can I save my own Instagram Story?

Instagram may offer built-in options for saving your own Story. Open the Story and check the options available in your app.

### Where does a downloaded Instagram Story go?

Check your browser's download history and your device's Downloads folder. On iPhone, a downloaded file may appear in the Files app rather than Photos.

### Can I repost a downloaded Story?

Downloading content does not grant permission to repost it. Get the creator's permission and follow applicable copyright law and platform rules.
