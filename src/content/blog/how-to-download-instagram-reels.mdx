The download location depends on your device and browser.

- **iPhone:** Check the Files app and its Downloads folder. If the video is stored there, open it and use the Share menu to save it to Photos when that option is available.

## Troubleshooting Instagram Reel downloads

### The Reel link does not work

Return to Instagram, open the original Reel, and copy its link again. Confirm that the post is still available and can be viewed normally.

### The downloader cannot process the Reel

The post may be private, restricted, deleted, or temporarily inaccessible. Try another publicly accessible Reel to help determine whether the problem is specific to that post.

### The download does not start

Check your internet connection, wait for processing to finish, and try again. If the issue persists, the source or service may not be available for that link.

### The video is missing from Photos or Gallery

Check the Downloads folder in your browser or file manager. Some browsers save files there instead of adding them directly to your photo library.

### The downloaded video will not play

Check whether the download finished successfully. If the file appears incomplete, try downloading it again if the link is still supported.
