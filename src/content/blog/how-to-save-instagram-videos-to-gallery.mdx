## 1. Saving a post inside Instagram is not the same as downloading it

Instagram lets you save posts so you can find them again inside the app. This does not place a video file in your phone's gallery.

## Frequently asked questions

### Does saving an Instagram post put it in my phone's gallery?

No. The bookmark feature saves the post inside Instagram. To create a local file, you need an available download option and must save the resulting file to your device.

### Where do downloaded Instagram videos go on an iPhone?

They may be in Safari's download list or the Downloads folder in Files. To put a compatible video in Photos, open the file and check the available Share actions.

### Where do downloaded Instagram videos go on Android?

They are often stored in the Downloads folder or the location configured by your browser. Check the browser's download history if you cannot find the file.

### Can I save videos from a private Instagram account?

Only access and download content you are authorized to view. A downloader may not support private content, and you should not try to bypass privacy restrictions.

### Why is there no Save Video option?

The option depends on the app, device, file type, and the features available for that post. If it is not offered, check the file in your Downloads folder or use another supported method that respects the creator's rights.

### Can I share a saved Instagram video elsewhere?

Downloading a video does not give you permission to repost or redistribute it. Get permission from the rights holder and follow applicable copyright law and platform rules.
