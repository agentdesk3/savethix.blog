Is your Instagram video download not working? The cause is usually one of a few things: the account is private, the post was removed, the link was copied incorrectly, Instagram requires a login for that content, or a temporary issue is getting in the way. This guide walks through practical checks to help you find the cause.

## 1. Check that the post is public

SaveThisX works with public Instagram posts and Reels. It does not sign in to Instagram.

- Open the post in a private or logged-out browser window.
- If you are asked to log in to see it, the link cannot be processed.
- If the account is private, the video cannot be downloaded.

Respect privacy settings and do not try to bypass access restrictions.

## 2. Check that the post still exists

A post can be deleted, archived, or restricted after you saved the link.

- Open the link in your browser to confirm that the video still plays.
- If Instagram shows an error or an unavailable page, the video cannot be processed.

## 3. Copy the link again

An incomplete link is a common reason for failure.

1. Open the Reel or video post in the Instagram app.
2. Tap **Share**.
3. Choose **Copy link**.
4. Open the downloader in your browser and paste the new link.
5. Make sure there is no extra text or space before or after the link.

## 4. Use a link to a single video

SaveThisX is built for links to individual public videos and Reels, such as links that contain `/p/` or `/reel/`. Profile pages and other types of links are not video links.

## 5. Try Analyze again, then try another public video

1. Refresh the downloader page.
2. Paste the link and select **Analyze** again.
3. If it still fails, test a different public Reel.

If another public Reel works, the problem is likely specific to the first post. If several public links fail, wait a while and try again, because Instagram sometimes changes how it serves media.

## 6. Check your connection

- Open another website to confirm your connection works.
- Switch between Wi-Fi and mobile data.
- Temporarily turn off a VPN if you use one.

## 7. Understand quality limits

The 1080p, 720p and 480p settings are configured maximum video heights, not guaranteed output resolutions. Best has no configured height ceiling. Actual output depends on the compatible source formats available for that video, so selecting a setting does not guarantee that resolution.

## 8. Find the downloaded file

The file may not be in your photo gallery.

- **iPhone:** Check the Downloads folder in the Files app, or Safari's download list.
- **Android:** Check your browser's download history and your file manager's Downloads folder.
- **Computer:** Check the Downloads folder configured in your browser.

The exact location depends on your browser and device.

## Frequently asked questions

### Why does one Instagram Reel download while another does not?

Posts differ in privacy, availability, and the media Instagram provides. A working link does not guarantee that every other link will work.

### Can I download videos from private Instagram accounts?

No. SaveThisX only supports public content and does not use Instagram credentials.

### Why does the link work in the Instagram app but not in the downloader?

The app can use your signed-in account. SaveThisX does not, so content that needs a login cannot be processed.

### Do I need to install an app?

No. SaveThisX runs in your browser on iPhone, Android, and desktop.

### Is it okay to repost a downloaded video?

Downloading does not give you permission to republish someone else's work. Get permission from the creator and follow copyright law and platform rules.
