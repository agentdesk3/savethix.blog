## What to know before downloading

Not every TikTok video can be downloaded in the same way. A creator's settings, a post's privacy, regional restrictions, or changes to the source can affect whether a download works.

A watermark-free result is not guaranteed for every link. Only download videos you have permission to save, and get the creator's permission before reusing or republishing their work.

## Method 1: Check TikTok's built-in download option

1. Open TikTok and find the video you want to save.
2. Tap **Share**.
3. Look for **Save video**, if that option is available.
4. Wait for the download to finish.
5. Open Photos or your device's gallery and check the saved video.

The built-in option may not be available for every post. Also, saving a video through TikTok does not necessarily mean the resulting file will be watermark-free.

## What if the downloaded video still has a watermark?

Try these checks:

- **Check the selected output:** Review the options actually offered by the downloader. Do not assume an option exists if it is not shown.
- **Copy the link again:** Return to the original TikTok post and copy its link again.
- **Check the source video:** Make sure the post is still available and can be opened normally.
- **Try another supported public video:** This can help determine whether the issue is specific to one post.
- **Avoid suspicious download pages:** Do not install unknown apps, browser extensions, or files just to remove a watermark.

If the available download still contains a watermark, the service may not be able to provide a watermark-free version of that video.

## Where is the downloaded video saved?

The location depends on your device and browser.

- **iPhone:** Check the Files app, especially the Downloads folder. If the file is there, open it and use the Share menu to save it to Photos when that option is available.
- **Android:** Check your browser's download list or the Downloads folder in your file manager.
- **Computer:** Check the Downloads folder configured in your browser.

If the file is missing, open your browser's download history and check whether the download completed.
